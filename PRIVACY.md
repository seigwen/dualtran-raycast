# Privacy Policy — DualTran for Raycast

DualTran translates text you explicitly select or have on the clipboard. It
collects **nothing**: no analytics, no telemetry, no accounts, no developer
server.

- Text you translate is sent to the service **you** selected: Google (default,
  free, no account) or, in AI mode, directly to your AI provider with your own
  API key.
- Preferences (including the API key, when set) are stored by Raycast locally
  on your Mac.
- No other network requests are made.

Bundled engine: `@dualtran/core` (MPL-2.0), extracted from
https://github.com/seigwen/DualTran-extension.
